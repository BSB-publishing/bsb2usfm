### Why Public Domain?

The Berean Standard Bible project believes that God's Word should be freely accessible to all people without legal barriers or restrictions. By dedicating the text to the public domain, we ensure:

- **Universal Access** - Anyone can use it without legal concerns
- **No Gatekeeping** - No permission process or licensing fees
- **Maximum Freedom** - Use in any context, including commercial
- **Perpetual Availability** - Cannot be "taken back" or restricted later

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to credit the BSB when I use the text?
**A:** No, attribution is not required (but is appreciated).

### Q: Can I use the BSB text in a commercial product?
**A:** Yes, absolutely. No fees or royalties.

### Q: Can I modify the Bible text?
**A:** Yes, you can edit, revise, or adapt it as needed.

### Q: Do I need permission to use the BSB in my project?
**A:** No permission is required for the Bible text.

### Q: Can I claim copyright on the BSB text?
**A:** No, the BSB text is in the public domain and cannot be copyrighted.

### Q: What about translations of the BSB into other languages?
**A:** You're free to translate. Your translation can have its own copyright, but the original BSB text remains public domain.

### Q: If I use the Python conversion scripts, what do I need to do?
**A:** Include the MIT license notice if you distribute the scripts. Using the scripts to generate Bible files doesn't require attribution for the output files.

### Q: Can I use the BSB text in my church/ministry/app/website?
**A:** Yes! Use it freely without restriction.

### Q: What if my jurisdiction doesn't recognize public domain?
**A:** The UNLICENSE file provides fallback permissive licensing for such jurisdictions.

### Q: Can I bundle the BSB with other Bible translations?
**A:** Yes, though be aware of the licensing terms of the other translations.

### Q: Is the BSB text really free forever?
**A:** Yes. Public domain dedication is irrevocable. The BSB text will remain free forever.
